# Red-team review 2026-11-13: findings and fixes

An independent red-team review of the tree at `27e9d2c` (five area reviewers plus a lead
verification pass, static only) found no Critical and no way for an unprivileged actor to move
funds out of the policy wallet, bypass its caps, or take owner authority on chain. It found 3
High, 7 Medium and 22 Low or Info issues around the contract: in the paid API, the hosted demo,
the engine, the SDK, CI and the documentation. This page records what changed for each one.
The review's own reports stay with the operator; this is the public record.

Every fix below has a regression test. The test names are in the table.

## High

| ID | Finding | Fix | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| RT-01 | `POST /reservations` took the price and the account from the request, so a full scan could be reserved for 1 atomic unit, and no job was bound to its caller | The server charges the price-book price of the SKU its scan route runs; another amount is a 400 (the mock provider keeps a non-zero development override). Jobs and accounts are bound to the caller that created them; anyone else gets the same 404 as for an id never issued. The x402 price follows the scan SKU | `near_reservations_are_priced_by_the_server`, `jobs_and_accounts_are_bound_to_their_caller`, `reservation_price_ignores_a_cheaper_caller_amount_outside_dev` |
| RT-02 | The hosted demo's Approve button signed `approve_payment` with the owner key for any visitor, so on a wallet that holds unknown payees, the visitor who queued an injected payment could also release it | A visitor can approve only a held payment to the `config.json` allowlisted payee within its per-call cap. Any other held payment needs `TRIAGEPAY_OWNER_ACTION_TOKEN`. Reject stays public (it moves no funds) | `RT-02: a visitor cannot approve a held payment to an unknown payee` |
| RT-03 | `POST /api/policy` with `TRIAGEPAY_POLICY_APPLY=1` turned a sentence into a signed `set_agent` / `set_review_policy` in one request | Apply needs the flag and the owner token, the `confirm` digest of the exact previewed change set, and a change inside the committed bounds (payees from the `config.json` allowlist, caps no looser) | `RT-03: apply is bounded and bound to the previewed change set`, `apply stays off unless the flag is set` |

## Residuals

- The hosted project still has no shared KV store; the per-address limits are per instance. The
  allowance floor and the contract's own caps are the bounds that hold across instances.
- The deployed contracts are immutable by design. A future contract change ships as a new
  factory and new wallets, never as an upgrade.
- The paid API binds loopback and its ledger is process-local; caller authentication is still a
  later milestone, so the new job binding is exercised with one caller per server today.
