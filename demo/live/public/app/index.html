Create a wallet on mainnet.

Beta, unaudited, use small amounts. I have not had this contract audited. A raw ft_transfer can move tokens without the deposit cap the wallet enforces on ft_transfer_call.

The create cost is a 1 NEAR fee plus 2.52906 NEAR of refundable storage. The storage is the wallet code (221,906 bytes, 2.21906 NEAR), 0.01000 NEAR of state, and a 0.30000 NEAR working balance so a few holds do not fail with LackBalanceForState. You sign create_wallet on triagepay.hlpok1lad.tg. Anything above that total comes back at once. The fee goes to hlpok1lad.tg only after new succeeds. A failed create refunds the fee and the storage.

The storage comes back when you pause the wallet, withdraw every token, and call close with a beneficiary account that already exists. That deletes the wallet and returns the code stake, the state, and the working balance.

Circle native USDC on NEAR is 17208628f84f5d6ad33f0da3bbbeb27ffcb398eac501a31bd6ad2011e36133a1 (6 decimals). Wrapped NEAR is wrap.near. The beta deposit ceiling is 100 USDC and 10 wNEAR. You can set a lower ceiling. You cannot raise it past that.

Each wallet gets its own copy of the code. There is no global contract on this deploy. The factory account itself locks about 2.5 NEAR for its code, and that stays until the factory account is deleted.
